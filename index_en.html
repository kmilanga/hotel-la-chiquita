Our rooms are your base for living the authentic Córdoban experience. Located in a 16th-century palace in the heart of the Jewish Quarter, they combine historic charm with modern comforts, designed for travellers who seek to immerse themselves in local culture — not just observe it.

GASTRONOMY

Gastrotaberna La Chiquita de Quini

Enjoy traditional Córdoban cuisine in our historic courtyard. A sensory journey through recipes that have endured for generations.

From the most authentic salmorejo to the aromas of Sephardic cooking, every dish is served beneath the wooden coffered ceiling of our palace.

From your windows you will hear the strumming of flamenco guitars, the lively conversations of the Andalusian courtyard, and feel the vibration of "La Chiquita de Quini" — our acclaimed restaurant with live shows (Wednesday–Sunday, 16:00–01:00).

We operate with an autonomous code-based access system, giving you complete freedom over your arrival time.

  • 3–5 days before arrival: You will receive your access codes for the palace and your room via email/WhatsApp.
  • On arrival: Enter the code at the main entrance and go straight to your room.
  • 24/7 availability: We are reachable by WhatsApp and phone for any assistance.

Check-in: From 14:00.

Check-out: Before 12:00.

If you need early check-in or late check-out, contact us in advance. Subject to availability, we may offer it at no extra charge or with a small supplement.

Luggage storage: Available at no charge if you arrive before 14:00 or leave after 12:00.

We do not have our own car park. The Jewish Quarter is a pedestrian/restricted traffic zone and you cannot load/unload luggage at the hotel entrance.

Recommended public car parks nearby:

  • Parking Mezquita-Catedral — 200 m (€19/day)
  • Parking La Ribera — 700 m (€19/day)
  • Free car park — 1,500 m
