WE ANSWER YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does check-in work?

We operate with an autonomous code-based access system. On the day of your arrival you will receive the codes needed to enter both the palace and your room, giving you complete freedom over your arrival time.

Is there a 24-hour front desk?

We do not have a continuous physical reception. However, we are reachable 24 hours a day via WhatsApp and phone for any assistance you need during your stay.

Is it a quiet area?

We are at the epicentre of Córdoba's leisure scene. In the evenings and at night you can enjoy the atmosphere and live music at our Gastrotaberna La Chiquita de Quini. Ideal for those who want to experience the vibrant pulse of the city.

Do you offer breakfast?

We do not serve breakfast at the hotel, but our privileged location lets you enjoy the best cafés and pastry shops in the Jewish Quarter just a few steps away.

Check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is before 12:00.
