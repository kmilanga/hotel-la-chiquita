2. Data we collect and how we obtain it

2.1 Bookings through our website

When you make a booking through the booking engine integrated on our website (managed by ACI Group), the data you provide — name, surname, email, phone number, stay dates and payment method — is processed directly by ACI Group as data processor acting on our behalf. Please also refer to their privacy policy.

2.2 Communications via WhatsApp and telephone

When you contact us via WhatsApp or phone, we process the data you voluntarily provide (name, enquiry, phone number) solely to handle your request.

2.3 Website browsing (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to analyse visitor behaviour on our website in an aggregated and anonymous manner. This service only places analytical cookies if you have given your consent through the cookie banner. You may withdraw your consent at any time via our Cookie Policy.

3. Purposes and legal bases for processing

Managing bookings and staysPerformance of the accommodation contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR)
Customer service via WhatsApp/phoneLegitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6.1.f GDPR)
Website statistical analysis (GA4)User consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR)
Compliance with legal obligations (invoicing, traveller registration)Legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c GDPR)

4. Recipients and international transfers

Data is not shared with third parties except in the following cases:

— ACI Group: data processor for booking engine management.
— Google LLC (Google Analytics 4): web analytics provider. Google may transfer collected data to servers in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
— Public authorities: when required by applicable law (e.g. communication of traveller data to law enforcement agencies).

5. Retention periods

Booking and stay data is retained for 5 years from the end of the contractual relationship, in accordance with applicable tax and commercial obligations.

WhatsApp/phone communication data is retained for the time necessary to handle the enquiry and, where applicable, during the limitation period for potential claims (3 years).

Analytical data collected by Google Analytics 4 is retained for 14 months under the tool's standard configuration.

6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time by sending an email to administracion@hotelachiquita.es with a copy of your identity document:

— Access: find out what data we process about you.
— Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
— Erasure: request deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary.
— Restriction: request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
— Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
— Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
— Withdrawal of consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

If you consider that the processing of your data does not comply with applicable regulations, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence within the EU.

7. Security

We apply the necessary technical and organisational measures to ensure the security of your data and prevent its alteration, loss, processing or unauthorised access, taking into account the state of technology, the nature of the data and the risks to which it is exposed.

8. Changes to this policy

La Chiquita de Quini S.L. reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy to adapt to regulatory or service changes. The current version will always be available at this URL.
