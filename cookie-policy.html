Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use on www.hotelachiquita.es and how you can manage them. We respect your privacy: non-essential cookies are only activated with your consent.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They help the site work correctly, remember your preferences or analyse how the site is used.

2. Types of cookies by purpose

Technical or strictly necessary cookies: Essential for the website to function. They do not require consent.

Analytical cookies: Allow us to understand how users interact with the site in order to improve it. They require prior consent.

Preference cookies: Remember user choices (such as language). In our case, we store your cookie banner decision in your browser's local storage (localStorage), not via cookies.
